# Content Guidelines

Writing rules for all public-facing copy on the HNM Professional Services website.

## General Voice

Content should feel:

- Warm
- Dependable
- Compassionate
- Plainspoken
- Professional
- Local and personal

## Writing Style

- Use short paragraphs
- Use descriptive headings
- Lead with client outcomes
- Avoid jargon
- Avoid exaggerated claims
- Avoid empty marketing language
- Use active voice
- Use direct calls-to-action
- Use the business name consistently: **HNM Professional Services**
- Use the approved tagline exactly when included: **Supporting People, Cherishing Pets.**

## CTA Language

Preferred primary CTA:

- Request a Free Consultation

Acceptable supporting CTAs:

- Explore Services
- Learn About HNM
- Read Client Reviews
- Contact HNM
- Call Now

Avoid:

- Buy Now
- Book Now (unless true scheduling is implemented)
- Instant Booking
- Guaranteed Availability
- Medical or clinical claims

## Service Copy Safety

### Senior companion care

- Do not imply licensed medical care
- Do not imply nursing
- Do not imply emergency response
- Do not promise health outcomes
- Clearly describe nonmedical companionship and support

### Pet services

- Do not claim certifications or special qualifications unless confirmed
- Do not promise availability
- Avoid veterinary or medical claims
- Do not publish “boutique” or specialty positioning until the client approves final wording

### House sitting

- Do not imply security guarantees
- Do not promise prevention of theft, damage, or emergencies
- Describe care and monitoring in practical, honest terms

## Placeholder Policy

Never invent:

- Credentials
- Years of experience
- Service areas
- Testimonials
- Contact information
- Business hours
- Certifications
- Insurance coverage
- Review ratings

Use obvious placeholders such as:

- `[CLIENT PHONE]`
- `[CLIENT EMAIL]`
- `[APPROVED SERVICE REGION]`
- `[CLIENT BIO REQUIRED]`
- `[APPROVED TESTIMONIAL]`
- `[BUSINESS HOURS]`

Placeholders must remain clearly marked until replaced with client-approved content. Do not publish placeholders as if they were final copy.
