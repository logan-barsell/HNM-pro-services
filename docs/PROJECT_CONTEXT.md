# Project Context

## Business

- **Business name:** HNM Professional Services
- **Tagline:** Supporting People, Cherishing Pets.
- Small startup offering local, trust-based care services
- The website should feel warm, dependable, professional, and approachable

HNM Professional Services helps people care for what matters most—pets, aging loved ones, and homes—while building confidence through clear communication and reliable service.

## Core Services

### Pet Sitting & Dog Walking

In-home pet care and walking for owners who need dependable support while they are away or busy. The business has discussed specializing in small or boutique pets, but that positioning and final wording must be verified with the client before publishing.

### Companion Care for Seniors

Nonmedical companionship and everyday support for seniors. Site copy must **not** imply medical care, nursing care, emergency care, or regulated personal-care services unless the client confirms qualifications and service boundaries.

### House Sitting & Home Checks

Nontechnical monitoring and care of a client’s home while they are away—checking that the property is secure, cared for, and attended to as agreed. Final service details, visit frequency, and inclusions must come from the client.

## Primary Website Goal

Encourage qualified prospective clients to **request a free consultation** before booking services.

## Secondary Goals

- Explain services clearly
- Establish credibility
- Build trust
- Show reviews and testimonials
- Present service regions
- Make phone and email contact easy

## Audience

Likely audiences include:

- Pet owners seeking reliable sitting or walking support
- Seniors and their family members looking for nonmedical companionship
- Homeowners who travel and want peace of mind about their property
- People seeking a dependable local service provider rather than a large franchise

## Conversion Strategy

The primary call to action should use language such as:

**“Request a Free Consultation”**

Do not imply that submitting the form creates a confirmed appointment unless a scheduling system is later implemented. The consultation request is the first step toward understanding needs and availability.

## Tone

Use language that is:

- Warm
- Reassuring
- Respectful
- Clear
- Professional
- Not overly corporate
- Not childish
- Not overly clinical
